# Archive buyer research: selection evidence, not demand proof

Use this method when archived community discussions inform content topics, buyer questions or service-page improvements. It supplements [semantic acceptance](../SEMANTIC_ACCEPTANCE.md); it does not change the scoring rubric, replace the existing workflow or authorize publication, collection, spending, outreach or scheduling. All examples below are synthetic; no observed business or community data is included.

## Start from the decision and outcome

Load the user's private business context: ICP, offer, scope, fee evidence, approved proof and relevant destinations. State the buyer decision and eligible KPI, such as qualified inquiries or accepted proposals. A post, click, company revenue figure or operator question does not establish a qualified buyer, available budget or authority. Keep unsupported fields UNKNOWN.

Use two complementary content lanes:

- **EXPERT_INSIGHT:** original diagnosis, defensible point of view, tradeoffs, approved firsthand proof, a useful worked artifact, limits and counterexamples. Do not assert a top-percentile expertise ranking.
- **BUYER_DECISION:** fit, scope, cost, timeline, responsibility, alternatives, implementation versus advice, relevant proof and the next service step. Avoid generic trend roundups and rewrites of archived posts.

## Build a bounded evidence sample

Choose communities using relevant accepted examples and audience fit, not popularity. Record the source method, query filters, UTC as-of and capture time, sample cap, failures and provenance hashes. Use an existing authorized collector or private export; do not install an unreviewed repository or infer its license permits reuse. Provider permissions, cost and rate limits remain separate decisions. No automatic retries or challenge bypass.

Latest 7, 14 and 30 days overlap. Derive disjoint age strata `(0,7]`, `(7,14]`, `(14,30]` days, with an end-exclusive as-of instant. Record exact boundaries. Never sum nested-window results, equate capped latest-first samples with a population, or infer growth when caps, filters, communities or missing strata differ.

Deduplicate post IDs across windows while retaining observations. Group repeated content and repeated sellers separately from independent buyer evidence. Record archive freshness; verify selected primary sources live. Removed/deleted content is excluded from recommendations. Blocked or unverified primary content remains a candidate, not verified demand. Do not reconstruct deleted material from an older archive.

Classify buyer, operator question, seller/promotion or UNKNOWN. Cluster the job to be done, trigger, objection, consequence and negative cases. Budget remains UNKNOWN without explicit evidence, and a self-report is not independently verified purchasing power. Validate proposed solutions against current primary method sources: a question does not establish the best answer.

## Connect evidence without inventing joins

Use genuine native query-and-page pairs from the latest 14 complete Search Console Pacific days, or UNKNOWN. Explicit incomplete-date metadata or equivalent native final-data proof must establish completeness; missing rows are not zero. Do not join separate query totals and page totals to manufacture a pair.

Keep qualified-path analytics aggregates, explicit event-source evidence and identity separate. A click is not a booking or qualified meeting. Record the distinction between UTC research windows and Pacific search-data windows. Keep raw queries, source bodies, handles, business identifiers, fees and customer evidence private.

## Select the smallest adequate intervention

Read whole current content, including existing guides, FAQs/schema and CTAs. Compare actual buyer decisions and original artifacts, not keyword presence. A short service paragraph is not automatically a full guide, and an existing full guide is not a new-page gap. Competitor evidence can reveal useful questions; missing detail is UNKNOWN, not a competitor grade.

Choose one:

- `NEW_BLOG`: a distinct, insufficiently covered buyer question with an original explanation or worked artifact.
- `NEW_GUIDE`: a distinct decision needing a reusable worksheet, process or comparison with truthful proof and limits.
- `REFINE_EXISTING`: an evidenced remaining gap in an existing answer; preserve approved claims, prices, scope and useful content.
- `NO_ACTION`: the question is already answered, evidence is weak, fit is wrong or the useful intervention is outside scope.

Each brief includes an original title, buyer question/JTBD, archive and live evidence references, native query-page evidence or UNKNOWN, current-coverage comparison, unique artifact/proof required, hub/service next step, locale, expected KPI and measurement plan. Examples may use synthetic placeholders, but must never appear as observed demand or client results.

Score ICE as `I*C*E/10`, with each factor from 1 to 10 and explicit reasons. Impact concerns the stated decision and KPI; confidence reflects live, method, native and coverage evidence; ease includes measured or bounded effort and verification cost. An unknown qualified-lead baseline stays UNKNOWN. A numeric score does not authorize publishing or establish a revenue forecast.

## Weekly memory and feedback

A separately authorized recurring process may keep private `runs/<UTC-week>/<unique-run>/` records and an aggregate index. Preserve per-post first-seen/last-seen observations and stable pattern IDs, evidence references, communities and independent-author evidence or UNKNOWN. Not observed this week is not deleted.

Use NEW_OBSERVED or REOBSERVED_IN_SAMPLE_UNVERIFIED for sample recurrence. Confirmed RECURRING requires reviewed evidence of at least three plausibly independent posts across at least two runs plus current primary checks; unknown authors do not establish independence. INCREASED or DECREASED requires comparable, uncapped populations and adequate eligible denominators; otherwise use INSUFFICIENT_COMPARABLE_DATA. Repeated sellers and different sample caps cannot establish demand growth.

Keep the existing semantic, source, native publication, CI, Preview, rights and public-verification gates mandatory. A research candidate is not published content. At actual public T0, evaluate at T0+7, +14 and +28 using final data, qualification lag, eligible denominators and concurrent-change controls. Retain, revise, reject or report insufficient data. Do not claim causal lift without a suitable design.

## Synthetic boundary example

A hypothetical archived question suggests a new guide. Whole-page review finds that the existing guide already supplies the same decision worksheet. The disposition is REFINE_EXISTING only if a specific remaining gap is evidenced; otherwise NO_ACTION. An additional archive mention does not prove growth, budget, independent buyers or a new page requirement.

Public exports contain only a separately reviewed anonymous summary. Never include raw posts, handles, real evidence identifiers, client routes, analytics identifiers or private operational receipts in this repository. This reference provides a method, not access to any private research store.
