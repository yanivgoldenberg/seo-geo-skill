# AI slop: research, patent and editorial application

Reviewed18 October2026. This is a source-disposition registry for content operations, not a legally exhaustive patent search or a Google ranking specification. The companion [primary-source register](primary-source-register.json) records source URLs, applicability and limitations.

## Practical conclusions

Google's documented Search policy targets scaled low-value manipulation, regardless of how text is produced. Useful AI assistance is not categorically prohibited. Assess the actual reader task, original contribution, factual support and truthful delivery. [Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies), [people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content), [generative AI content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content), [AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide).

The [supplied S-CTS paper](https://storage.googleapis.com/gweb-research2023-media/pubtools/1039291.pdf) and [SAFE companion](https://storage.googleapis.com/gweb-research2023-media/pubtools/1039292.pdf) address coordinated synthetic-media abuse on video platforms. They motivate combining contextual evidence and human review. They do not establish a website AI-writing penalty. A legitimate CMS migration or reviewed batch release is not evidence of an abusive account network.

## Claim and citation checking

Use [FActScore](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.741/), [Knowledge-Based Trust](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.03519), [AIS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.12870), [attributed QA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08037) and [ALCE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14627) as methodological context: decompose claims and check whether identified sources support them. This is not a claim to run those published evaluators or reproduce their scores.

For case studies, separate role, personal implementation, wider team/company outcome, observation period and causal claim. For quantitative comparisons, identify metric, units, denominator, eligible cohort, period and source. A conversion count cannot be compared directly with a revenue amount. A historical company result is not a repeatable promise for a new client.

## Detector and optimization limits

[Detector reliability research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11156) and [non-native English bias research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.02819) explain why detector percentages cannot certify human authorship or quality. The bias findings do not themselves validate a Hebrew detector. Do not rewrite prose to evade a detector or treat watermark absence as proof of human authorship.

[GEO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735) reports strategy- and domain-dependent benchmark effects. Its maximum reported visibility gain is not a current-engine or customer forecast. [Lost in the Middle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172) motivates keeping an answer and its qualifications easy to find; it does not establish a preferred website length or a current engine's retrieval window.

## Corrections to circulated claims

- US20180150752A1 is assigned to Newsrx LLC, not Google. US12322402B2 is an individual-assigned music patent. CN115803808A is a Microsoft speech-detection record. Citing a Google patent does not make an invention Google-owned.
- US9582586B2, sometimes surfaced by broad searches, is originally assigned to Wal Mart Stores Inc. It is excluded from the Google set.
- S-CTS reference6 prints a Harvard Law Review URL that resolves to *Medical Disobedience* by Dov Fox, not the named speech-governance work. Preserve this link mismatch in source review; do not repeat the wrong citation as verified.
- SynthID watermarking, C2PA credentials, classifier outputs, spam enforcement and Search ranking answer different questions. Do not infer a universal public text detector API from a portal, sample developer architecture or partner service.
- Cosine distance, unusual vocabulary, entropy and publishing pace are not validated universal quality scores. Do not inject invisible canaries or hidden text into customer pages or vary language to manipulate detectors.
- Google's FAQ rich-result feature ended in May2026; valid FAQ content/markup is not a promise of those results. Current documentation is recorded in [Search updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates).
- [Ahrefs' llms.txt study](https://ahrefs.com/blog/llmstxt-study/) concerns137,210 customer domains with May2026 traffic. The97% zero-request figure refers to valid llms.txt files, not every domain. File fetching is not proof that an engine uses the file for ranking or answers.

## Applying the reader review

Use the per-page checklist in [Phase22](../../seo-geo.md#phase-22---content-integrity-and-the-reader-review). Record actual purpose, relevant detail, scan structure, language, sources, interaction behavior, mobile/keyboard/RTL evidence and next step. Automatic measurements supply evidence for review; they do not certify comprehension, ADHD suitability or factual completeness. Complete inventory coverage is not the same as passing every criterion.

Keep before/after populations and extraction methods identical. Preserve reviewed exceptions and failed attempts. A source-only fix, a local browser check, a protected candidate, a public deployment and later commercial outcomes require separate evidence.
