# Expert content: from an attributable source to a useful decision

Use this reference when an approved expert source becomes a buyer decision aid or distinct distribution drafts. Extend the current brief and claim register; do not create another content operating system. Retain [semantic acceptance](../SEMANTIC_ACCEPTANCE.md), existing source reviews, page cooldowns, applicable workflow stages and native release gates. This is documentation: it installs no runtime validator, scheduler, publishing integration or permission to send.

## Make eight existing practices observable

| Practice | Minimum useful handoff | Acceptance observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attributable expert proof | Original problem, personal contribution, diagnosis, intervention, source limits and one literal usable artifact | A reader can use the artifact and identify a limitation. An interview remains self-report unless independently corroborated. |
| Buying roles and objections | Buyer role, decision or objection, proof needed, decision aid and appropriate next action | Identify source-backed findings separately from interview hypotheses. A role or job title does not prove authority, budget or need. |
| Claim and voice capsule | Claim ID, exact approved text, source span and hash, source date, ownership/rights, limits, factual locks, approved voice example and forbidden expansion | Every material derivative claim traces to approved support. Rewriting cannot silently change fees, currency, responsibility, attribution or locale meaning. |
| Reader task | Existing destination, intended decision, expected artifact use and one appropriate next action | Read the whole composition and check that the reader can complete that task. Keyword coverage alone is insufficient. |
| Distinct derivatives | Source claim IDs, channel, angle, useful content and canonical destination | Each draft adds a different reason to read it. Prefer fewer useful assets to repetitive summaries; no posting quota or algorithm claim follows. |
| Explicit review closure | Exact location, defect, proposed correction, reviewer, resolution and rechecked version | Retain failed attempts. Approval applies to the final reviewed copy; changed copy or source requires affected checks again. |
| Actual publication | Named publisher, approved copy hash, actual URL/date and native readback | Draft, Approved and Published remain separate. A local file, share button, scheduled row or accepted API request alone does not establish public delivery. |
| Qualified outcomes | Defined outcome, population, window, unit, denominator, exclusions, join limits and next decision | Keep bookings, attendance, qualification, opportunities, signed fees and collected revenue separate. Missing measurements remain UNKNOWN. |

Choose one rights-cleared firsthand source and improve an appropriate existing answer before creating another URL. Produce a literal decision aid, such as a troubleshooting tree or evidence worksheet. Only produce a walkthrough when it helps the task: specify spoken claim, on-screen support, timing, accessible text and one relevant next action. A storyboard is a plan; real rendering, rights and publication require their own evidence. Sales reuse requires the actual recipient and sending authority.

## Carry four compact blocks in the existing record

Where the owning workflow implements an `editorial-handoff/1` extension, the intended location is the existing page method, not a separate database. The four blocks below describe the portable method. Consult that workflow's accepted validator for exact serialized requirements; this public skill does not execute or certify the extension.

1. `capsules[]`: reference existing claim/proof IDs, exact approved text and source evidence hash, a precise locator, rights, limits and factual locks. A matching hash identifies bytes, not truth or permission. Retain source date, contribution/ownership and approved voice guidance in the linked source/claim record.
2. `buyer`: record the role and objection as UNKNOWN, HYPOTHESIS or SUPPORTED. Present an untested hypothesis as a hypothesis. Connect the decision, required proof, useful artifact and appropriate next action; retain a reasoned NO_ACTION when the existing answer suffices.
3. `derivatives[]`: bind each angle and reader task to the selected claim IDs, exact copy and copy hash. Review binds the current source hashes and locks to that copy. A Published claim additionally needs the actual URL, publication time and native readback evidence. Record issue location and resolution in the linked review artifact; a checklist label cannot replace it.
4. `assessment`: reference the existing observed outcome stage and decision, keeping missing metrics UNKNOWN. Report the denominator's unit, value and verification state. Preserve the reporting window, cohort, eligibility, deduplication and unresolved joins in the linked measurement record.

Optional distribution work does not make social posts or email mandatory for every page. Native channel authority, consent, suppression, receiver reconciliation and actual delivery remain separate. No source or generic method grants rights to publish a client's material. Keep private sources, identities, offers, fees, operational receipts and analytics identifiers out of public exports; publish only separately reviewed anonymous examples.

## Synthetic example: useful differences, honest limits

The following is fictional drafting input, not evidence of customer demand or a historical result:

> In a synthetic diagnostic exercise, event counts and booking confirmations use different reporting windows. The supplied worksheet marks their join as unresolved.

The approved claim is only that the synthetic exercise illustrates a window mismatch and unresolved join. Its source locator identifies that exact sentence. It does not establish real lost revenue, improved conversion or the cause of a customer's problem.

The buyer decision is which evidence must be reconciled before changing acquisition spend. A finance reviewer's concern about double counting is an interview hypothesis until supported. The artifact is a worksheet with source, reporting window, observed count or UNKNOWN, eligible unit, verification state, join evidence and next diagnostic action.

Three possible draft angles are a mistaken diagnosis, a worked reconciliation mechanism and a decision checklist. Each must include useful content, inherit the same source limits and point to the appropriate existing answer. If two drafts repeat the same argument, combine or omit one. Do not invent another result to make them different.

A reviewer flags the sentence "the worksheet recovered revenue" as unsupported, identifies its exact location, and replaces it with a statement about the fictional exercise. The final copy hash and source bindings are rechecked. Even after approval, its distribution status remains Approved until actual authorized publication and readback. A new copy edit invalidates the previous copy-bound approval.

The outcome decision remains INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE while the qualified-opportunity denominator or reliable joins are unavailable. An unknown booking count is not zero. This example contributes no publication, qualified lead or revenue result.

## Review usefulness and business evidence separately

Predeclare qualification criteria and the intended mechanism. Review retain, revise, reject or insufficient evidence at the existing observation horizons; respect cooldowns and outcome maturation. Retain a useful artifact on supported task evidence, revise a demonstrated task failure, and reject unsupported or redundant content. Do not call a commercial winner from a few posts or before/after counts with changing traffic mix.

Use accepted unique qualified opportunities divided by a declared eligible population in a fixed window when those data exist. Keep person, account, visitor and session units distinct; repeated sessions are not independent people. Report pipeline currency, stage, valuation convention and deduplication before totaling it. Pipeline and collected revenue cannot be added as one outcome. When identity or attribution joins are unavailable, report unjoined outcomes and attribution UNKNOWN.

Relevant visits, verified inquiries, factual defects, reopened issues and production/review hours are useful supporting measures. Likes and opens are secondary signals. Do not reweight a score to hide missing conversions. Without a valid causal design, report associations and limitations rather than lift caused by content.

## Primary guidance and its limits

- [Google people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) supports original value and visible expertise. It is publisher guidance, not a conversion forecast or word-count requirement.
- [Google AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) retains ordinary search eligibility and does not guarantee inclusion. Aggregate Web reporting does not isolate qualified demand from AI appearances.
- [W3C PROV overview](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-overview/) explains source, activity and responsibility relationships. Provenance helps assessment; it does not prove correctness. No new provenance database is required here.
- [NIST generative AI profile](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf) supports source/citation verification and careful interpretation of generated claims. It is voluntary risk guidance, not evidence of content ROI.
- [Microsoft metric-interpretation research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/2017-08-KDDMetricInterpretationPitfalls.pdf) documents experimentation pitfalls. Transfer population and metric checks; large-product results do not establish a consultancy's sample requirements or expected effect.
- [NIST proportion-interval guidance](https://itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc241.htm) explains uncertainty for appropriate binomial samples. Dependent visits or shifting populations can violate those assumptions; an interval alone establishes no causation.

These sources inform the method, not a ranking, citation, qualified-lead or revenue guarantee. The existing Phase 0-15 scoring rubric is unchanged.
